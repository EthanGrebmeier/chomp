Chomp ("Chomp," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by {{LEGAL_ENTITY}}, provides a
grocery list, recipe, and meal-planning application (the "App"). This Privacy
Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with,
and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described
in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide

- **Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email
  address. Depending on how you sign in, this is provided through Apple Sign In,
  Google Sign In, or an email magic code.
- **Content you create.** We store the content you add to the App, including
  grocery lists and list items, saved items, recipes and recipe ingredients,
  imported recipe source URLs, meal plans, stores, and custom categories.
- **Recipe images.** If you choose to add an image to a recipe, you grant the
  App access to your device's photo library so you can select that image. We
  only access photos you explicitly choose.
- **Recipe imports.** When you import a recipe from the web, you provide a URL.
  We send that URL to our recipe-parsing service to extract ingredient and
  recipe details (see "Third-Party Services" below).

### Information collected automatically

- **Authentication and session data.** To keep you signed in and secure your
  account, we and our authentication provider process authentication tokens and
  session identifiers.
- **Device and local storage.** The App stores data locally on your device
  (including a local database, cached content, and securely stored credentials)
  to enable offline use and faster performance.
- **Technical and diagnostic data.** We may process limited technical
  information such as app version, device type, and network status to operate,
  maintain, and troubleshoot the App.

### Guest use

You can use the App as a guest without creating a full account. In guest mode we
create a temporary, anonymous session so your content can be stored and synced.
If you later sign in, your guest content may be associated with your account.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features.
- Create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
- Store and synchronize your content across your devices in real time.
- Enable collaborative features, such as sharing a grocery list with others.
- Parse recipes you import from the web.
- Diagnose problems, improve performance, and develop new features.
- Protect the security and integrity of the App and prevent abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

## 3. Shared Lists and Collaboration

Chomp lets you share a grocery list with other users by inviting them with a
join code. When you share a list, the people you share it with can view and
modify the shared list and its items. Do not include sensitive personal
information in shared lists. The owner of a list controls who has access to it.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party providers to deliver the App. These providers
process certain data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own
privacy policies:

- **Clerk** — user authentication and account management.
- **Apple Sign In** — sign-in using your Apple ID, where you choose it.
- **Google Sign In** — sign-in using your Google account, where you choose it.
- **InstantDB** — real-time database and synchronization of your content.
- **Expo** — application delivery and over-the-air updates.
- **Recipe parsing service** — processes recipe URLs you submit to extract
  recipe and ingredient information.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers to understand
how they handle your information.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the
following circumstances:

- **With service providers** who process data on our behalf to operate the App
  (see "Third-Party Services").
- **With other users** when you use collaborative features, such as sharing a
  grocery list.
- **For legal reasons**, when we believe disclosure is required to comply with
  applicable law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the
  rights, property, or safety of Chomp, our users, or others.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets,
  in which case we will take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains
  protected.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to
provide the App. If you delete content or your account, we will delete or
de-identify the associated data within a reasonable period, except where we are
required to retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or
enforce our agreements. Some data may remain in backups for a limited time.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

- **Access and update.** You can view and edit your content within the App.
- **Delete content.** You can delete grocery lists, recipes, meal plans, and
  other content you create.
- **Delete your account.** You may request deletion of your account and
  associated data by contacting us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
- **Photo access.** You can grant or revoke the App's access to your photo
  library in your device settings.
- **Sign out.** You can sign out of the App at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as
the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer
Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to access, correct, delete, or
restrict processing of your personal information, and the right to data
portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will
not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your
information, including encrypted storage of credentials on your device and
secure transmission of data. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age
required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
information, please contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} so we can take appropriate
action.

## 10. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than the one in
which you reside, including the United States. These countries may have data
protection laws that differ from those in your country. Where required, we take
steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material
changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate,
provide additional notice within the App. Your continued use of the App after
changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Duplicate-resolution exploration: reusable rules versus demo-only behavior

Reviewed `features/meal-planner/duplicate-resolution/{detection,planning,demo-choices}.ts`, its `README.md`, and `features/recipes/instant/stack-recipe-ingredients-plan.ts` after the owner's different-unit decision. This work is simulated and disconnected from production writes; reuse the **rules**, not its transaction assumptions.

- `detectDuplicates` matches same-name candidates on the destination list after trim/lowercase/whitespace normalization; excludes checked and deleted rows; includes incompatible candidates for review; and selects the newest matching-unit **and matching-store-ID** target. **Owner confirmed:** different shopping notes or categories do not block combining when name, unit and store match; keep the existing row's shopping metadata while preserving the incoming source's own label/notes. `planDuplicateResolution` allows only `separate` or a compatible `increment`, re-reads supplied current candidates at confirmation, and preserves incoming source identities in a _proposal_, not persisted entries. This is useful groundwork for the new entry-aware decision contract but **not** transaction-level isolation or complete reads.
- `demo-choices.ts` extends the presentation with same-unit/different-store choices (“combine at existing/incoming store”) and **different-unit custom-total** choices. These are explicitly UI-only and not executable through `planDuplicateResolution`. Remove the latter for production under the owner's decision; implement an entry-aware execution path for the former with “keep separate” as default, as the owner confirmed.
- The existing **live** recipe stacker `features/recipes/instant/stack-recipe-ingredients-plan.ts` coalesces ingredients and its `conflictResolution: 'increment'` can add into the newest same-name row despite incompatible units; it must be replaced or guarded before source-entry production activation. Do not confuse the safer simulated planner with today's live direct-recipe add path.

## Current schema and permission boundary

`instant.schema.ts` defines `grocery_items` with scalar `quantity`, `unit`, `name`, `category`, `notes`, `isChecked`, `isDeleted`, timestamps; links to `grocery_list`, `store`, `saved_item`, `recipe`, and one-to-one `meal_plan_ingredient_snapshot` or `meal_plan_item`. Snapshots have `quantity`, `unit`, `isSelected`, `isQuantityOverridden`, `sourceRecipeIngredientId`, and optional `store`; standalone planned items have their own quantity/unit/store/ignore flag. `instant.perms.ts` grants list members create/update/delete on whole `grocery_items`, `meal_plan_recipes`, `meal_plan_items`, and ingredient snapshot entities (their `isOwner` binding traverses the list's shares). `grocery_lists.update` also permits members, while `grocery_list_shares` has self/member rules. There is no local `attrs` policy restricting scalar fields or legacy source links for these writers. These are **local definitions**, not proof the deployed Instant app matches or that permission rules can distinguish old from new clients. There is no contribution entity or verified old-client write fence. Confirm deployed schema and permissions and test old and updated binaries before activation (#31, #44).

## Invariants to prove before selecting persistence (#43–45)

1. **Identity:** each manual add, recipe add operation × ingredient instance, scheduled snapshot instance (`meal_plan_recipe` + snapshot ID), or standalone `meal_plan_item` has a stable, unambiguous entry identity. Repeated direct adds remain distinct; retries must be idempotent for the same operation and must not collapse separate operations. Preserve provenance on source removal/history without inventing legacy allocations.
2. **Duplicate targeting:** only active unchecked, non-deleted rows on the destination list with normalized same name and compatible normalized unit are candidates; check row status again at commit, not just in demo detection. Matching is not fuzzy and never assumes conversions. **Owner confirmed** category/notes may differ without blocking combine; keep existing row shopping metadata and incoming entry source metadata. Store differences demand explicit row-store selection. Same-name separate rows remain distinct until the user explicitly selects a target; never silently consolidate migrated rows.
3. **Normalization and totals:** normalized unit equality is trim + case-fold + whitespace collapse (including empty-only-with-empty), not an alias/conversion. Persist a canonical row unit and preserve entry raw source unit. Aggregate = sum of **active** entry quantities for that row and unit, finite and validated; no independently writable scalar total. Decide policy for zero/empty entries before activation; do not treat old `quantity` as verified authoritative CAS or a write-safe projection.
4. **Read completeness:** a reconciler, move, merge or aggregate must know whether **all** entries and relevant checked/deleted/source history have loaded, including shared-list data. An empty/partial offline query is not proof that entries are absent. No destructive reconciliation or total rewrite on incomplete reads; validate server and SDK semantics, not only a local array length.
5. **Lifecycle:** source changes may update only active corresponding entries; scheduled deselection cannot be reversed by sync, checked/deleted purchases remain history, and removing a source must not remove another source's row. Owner confirmed a checked row remains at its purchased quantity with **no new delta entry** after its source quantity increases, and clearing a purchased row must not resurrect it. Specify what happens on later selection/new shopping cycle and when its source is deleted; #10's checked/deleted-linked-row-satisfies-reconciler invariant cannot simply be retained when several entries share a row.
6. **Migration and boundary:** one existing row → one raw editable entry, displayed like a manual quantity without a required legacy/unattributed label, preserving observed scalar total, unit, metadata, store, checked/deleted state, source links as historical context when not demonstrably attributable; optionally store origin unknown internally but never invent recipe provenance or silently consolidate rows. Count/total/link verification, resumability, backup/restore and permission fence are separate gates. #40 excludes old offline edit replay after cutover, **not** new-client offline/concurrent correctness. Archived revision-permission proposal (`36752a9`) is **not proven compare-and-swap**; no claim of transaction isolation until tested with actual InstantDB SDK and deployed rules.
